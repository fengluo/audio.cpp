This deployment converts the source BF16 weights to audio.cpp GGUF Q4_K.
It is an ASR artifact, not a Q4_K_M text model. Configuration and tokenizer
resources are embedded in the GGUF. No client-side conversion is required.
The original model authors retain their rights. This is a modified format
and quantization of their artifact. The distribution URL is not yet published.
